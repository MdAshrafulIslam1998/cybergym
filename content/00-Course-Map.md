## Operating principles

1. **Notes-first, not video-first.** Watch a video / read a chapter, then write the note in my own words. The note is the evidence I learned it.
2. **Every concept gets a public page.** No private cheat sheets. The act of writing for an audience is the forcing function.
3. **Every cert gets a folder.** Domain-by-domain notes, mistakes log, exam-day playbook.
4. **Every lab gets a writeup.** Even when it's easy. The writeup is the artifact, not the lab itself.
5. **AI is a study partner, not a substitute.** Use Claude to quiz me, summarize papers, suggest gaps — never to write the note for me from scratch.
